## B. Wiki-level checks

- **Tensions recorded, not contradictions:** `coord_flip()` is recommended for bar/dot labels but discouraged for boxplots — the book explains why (comparing vertical segments is harder). Density plots are "use sparingly" while violins (density-based) are fine "with sufficient data" — consistent once you read the conditions. Both are captured in [Coordinate systems](../ggplot2/coordinate-systems.md) and [Summary vs detail](../concepts/summary-vs-detail.md).
- **Ambiguity:** the book says a "second group of researchers *also* failed to replicate" the bank-to-45 result ([Talbot et al.](../people/talbot-gerth-hanrahan.md)); the first group is never identified.
- **Unverified secondary claims:** all claims about Cleveland–McGill, Tufte, Wilke and Talbot et al. come only from the book's text ([Open questions](../open-questions.md)).
- **Concept gaps (mentioned, no page):** *discrete color palettes / color for discrete variables*, *uncertainty / confidence bands* (only touched in [Smoothing](../concepts/smoothing.md)), *small multiples* as a general idea (only in [Faceting strategy](../concepts/faceting-strategy.md)), *trend types* (cycle vs long-term — covered inside [Time-series guide](../guides/time-series.md)). Not split out; revisit after the next ingest.
- **Mechanical lint:** `python3 tools/wiki.py lint` — clean at the time of writing (see [log](../log.md)).
