Statistician whose graphical-perception research underpins the book's theory. The book draws on four entries in `references.bib` (plus a named book).

## Contributions the book uses

- **Encoding/decoding view** of visualization: a plot succeeds only if decoding goes well → [Encoding and decoding](../concepts/encoding-and-decoding.md).
- **Cleveland & McGill (1985)** — experiment on how accurately people decode encodings of continuous data; yields the ten-item ranking → [Perceptual ranking](../concepts/perceptual-ranking.md). Cited in General strategy and Continuous-continuous.
- **Cleveland, McGill & McGill (1988)**, *The Shape Parameter of a Two-Variable Graph* — the "bank to 45" aspect-ratio result → [Aspect ratio and banking](../concepts/aspect-ratio-and-banking.md). Not replicated by [Talbot et al. (2012)](talbot-gerth-hanrahan.md), per the book.
- *The Elements of Graphing Data* (1994) — recommended reading in [Welcome](../sources/welcome.md).
- Cleveland (1993), *A Model for Studying Display Methods of Statistical Graphics* — in the bibliography but **not cited** in any chapter.
