Authors of *An Empirical Model of Slope Ratio Comparisons* (IEEE InfoVis, 2012; Stanford Vis group). The book cites it to say that a group of researchers **failed to replicate** the Cleveland, McGill & McGill (1988) "bank to 45" result, so banking to 45 is better treated as a heuristic than a rule → [Aspect ratio and banking](../concepts/aspect-ratio-and-banking.md), [William Cleveland](william-cleveland.md).

The book's wording ("A second group of researchers also failed to replicate…") is slightly ambiguous about who the *first* group is; the wiki records only the Talbot et al. citation. The paper is not in `raw/`; what it actually measured has not been checked ([Open questions](../open-questions.md)).
