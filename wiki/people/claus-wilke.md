Author of *Fundamentals of Data Visualization* (2019; free online at serialmentor.com/dataviz), a "wisdom" source named in [Welcome](../sources/welcome.md).

## Ideas credited to Wilke in the book

- **Legend order should match line order**, so decoding a color-coded plot needs less back-and-forth → [Direct labeling and legends](../concepts/direct-labeling-and-legends.md).
- **Size-encoded bubble plots** make it difficult to understand the relationship between the axis variables and the size variable → [Derived variables](../concepts/derived-variables.md), [Continuous-continuous guide](../guides/continuous-continuous-relationships.md).
- **The `stamp()` device** — tagging bad plots — is an idea "from Claus Wilke's Data Visualization" (comment in `_common.R`) → [Book build and data](../sources/book-build-and-data.md), [Bad-to-good catalog](../analyses/bad-to-good-catalog.md).

The book itself is not in `raw/`; it is the most useful source to add next ([Open questions](../open-questions.md)).
