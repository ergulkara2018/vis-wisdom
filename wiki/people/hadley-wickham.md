Creator of ggplot2 and author of *R for Data Science* (R4DS, with Garrett Grolemund), the book's companion for mechanics.

- **Wickham (2010), *A Layered Grammar of Graphics*** — cited for the plot elements (statistical transformation, position adjustment, coordinate system, scales) → [Grammar of graphics](../concepts/grammar-of-graphics.md).
- **R4DS** is linked throughout for prerequisite reading (data visualization, labels, annotations, factors, pipes) → [ggplot2 basics](../sources/ggplot2-basics.md).
- **Wickham (2016), *ggplot2: Elegant Graphics for Data Analysis*** — in the bibliography but **not cited** in any chapter.
