Author of *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* (2nd ed., 2001), one of the three "wisdom" sources named in [Welcome](../sources/welcome.md).

The book's one direct use: the quoted directive (p. 74) that graphics must answer **"Compared to what?"** → [Compared to what?](../concepts/compared-to-what.md), applied to bars vs dots on the 100 m final ([Zero baseline](../concepts/zero-baseline-for-bars.md)).

The book doesn't attribute any other specific rule to Tufte. The work itself is not in `raw/` ([Open questions](../open-questions.md)).
